# Official art

Every image in this folder, and the app icons and link preview in `client/`, is official
Nintendo art for *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* on Nintendo Switch 2: in-game icons,
renders, the English logo, key art and the map of Hyrule. © Nintendo. Files were downloaded once from Zelda Wiki's
galleries for the game, resized and converted to WebP/AVIF by `scripts/official-art.py`,
and are served from this site; nothing is hotlinked. This is a non-commercial fan project and is
not affiliated with Nintendo.

## Custom pieces

Drawn here because the remake has no usable asset at the needed size, in the same style:

- `icons/triforce.webp`: the remake's only gold Triforce is a 58px Threads of Time icon, so
  this is a redraw in its burnished-gold style. The README banners and dividers use the same shape.
- `client/icon-*.png`, `favicon.png`: the official Ocarina of Time icon on a plain night-green tile.
- `art/hyrule-map-1200.webp` is Nintendo's own "Stylized Map of Hyrule" for the remake, first shared by
  Nintendo on its social media and Japanese game site (Nintendo Life, 22 Sep 2026; Zelda Universe,
  14 Oct 2026). Zelda Wiki and Zelda Dungeon Wiki host the same 1980x1440 file; it is cropped here to
  trim empty parchment. It is the only full-overworld map the remake has published.
- The Map view's region hotspots and tints (`client/src/data/map.ts`): outlines traced over the
  official map above. The map itself is Nintendo's art; no N64, 3DS or ROM map is used.

## Audio

Nintendo publishes no downloadable recording of the Switch 2 remake's music or sound effects:
Zelda Wiki's remake galleries host no audio files, and the soundtrack only streams in the
Nintendo Music app, which can't be saved. Ripping a stream or trailer would break those
services' terms, so these sounds are original renditions made by `scripts/make-audio.py`.
The twelve songs use Koji Kondo's melodies (© Nintendo) performed by a synthesized ocarina
over a quiet harp, mono, faded at both ends so the site can crossfade them. They are not
the remake's recordings.

- `client/assets/audio/lullaby.{webm,m4a}`: Zelda's Lullaby
- `client/assets/audio/epona.{webm,m4a}`: Epona's Song
- `client/assets/audio/saria.{webm,m4a}`: Saria's Song
- `client/assets/audio/sun.{webm,m4a}`: Sun's Song
- `client/assets/audio/time.{webm,m4a}`: Song of Time
- `client/assets/audio/storms.{webm,m4a}`: Song of Storms
- `client/assets/audio/minuet.{webm,m4a}`: Minuet of Forest
- `client/assets/audio/bolero.{webm,m4a}`: Bolero of Fire
- `client/assets/audio/serenade.{webm,m4a}`: Serenade of Water
- `client/assets/audio/nocturne.{webm,m4a}`: Nocturne of Shadow
- `client/assets/audio/requiem.{webm,m4a}`: Requiem of Spirit
- `client/assets/audio/prelude.{webm,m4a}`: Prelude of Light
- `client/assets/audio/item-get.{webm,m4a}`: a short original "item get" style flourish, mono,
  2.4 s. Not the game's jingle.

If Nintendo releases the remake soundtrack for download, replace these and list the source here.
